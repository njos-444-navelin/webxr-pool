# Roadmap

Each phase ends in something playable or verifiable. The riskiest part, the interaction model, is exercised first.

| Phase | Deliverable                                                                                             | Playable result                                                     |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1     | Monorepo scaffold, docs, sim package with tests, UK 8-ball rules, game session interface                | Headless: tests pass, shots resolve correctly                       |
| 2     | Flat freestyle: placeholder table, balls, Roam / Bridge / Stroke with touch and mouse, local session    | Solo freestyle on desktop and phone, deployed for usability testing |
| 3     | VR freestyle: Enter VR, controllers, hand drop and cue, leash and haptics, in-session Babylon GUI menu  | Solo freestyle in a headset                                         |
| 4     | Rooms: Room Durable Object, shot relay, reconnect, shared links, join screen, avatars                   | Two people play from different devices                              |
| 5     | Voice: LiveKit, spatial audio, mute controls                                                            | People talk while they play                                         |
| 6     | Polish: sound effects, carpet and lamp arena, table model, tokens applied, quality tiers, link previews | The intended atmosphere                                             |
| 7     | Shell: lobby, profile, avatar editor, settings scopes with migration                                    | The product front door                                              |

Later, unscheduled: accounts and profile sync; more than two people per room including spectators who may take turns on a player's behalf; a competitive mode with turn timers; US 8-ball colour scheme; spin; arena customisation; snooker as a variant; native store packaging; per-room link preview images.

Status: phase 1 in progress (scaffold done, sim not started).
